A podcast player that stays out of your way. Search, subscribe, listen.

🎧 Background playback with lock screen and notification controls
⏩ Speed from 0.8x to 2x, and a skip step you choose
📝 Episode notes right under the player
🔍 Search the iTunes catalogue, with recent searches one tap away
🔔 Told about new episodes, unless you turn that off
📶 Opens with your shows already there, and says so plainly when you are offline
🎨 Material You colours, light or dark, a rail on tablets
✅ No account, no ads, no tracking. Open source.

Episodes stream. Saving them for offline listening is not supported yet.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

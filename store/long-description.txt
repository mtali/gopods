GoPods is a podcast player that stays out of your way. Search the iTunes catalogue,
subscribe to what you like, and listen.

LISTENING
- Background playback that keeps going after you leave the app
- Controls on your lock screen and in the notification shade
- Playback speed from 0.8x to 2x
- Skip forward and back by a step you choose, anywhere from 5 to 60 seconds
- Episode notes sit under the player, so you can read what an episode is about

FINDING SHOWS
- Search the iTunes podcast catalogue
- Recent searches are one tap away
- Subscribe, and your library becomes a grid of cover art

NEW EPISODES
- GoPods checks your subscriptions and tells you when there is something new
- Turn that off in settings if you would rather not be told

ON A POOR CONNECTION
- Your subscriptions and episode lists are kept on the device, so the app opens with
  content rather than a spinner
- Lose signal and GoPods says so instead of hanging, then loads on its own once you
  are back

FITS YOUR PHONE
- Material You, so colours follow your wallpaper on Android 12 and newer
- Light, dark, or follow the system
- Tablets and landscape get a navigation rail instead of a bottom bar

NO STRINGS
- No account
- No ads
- No analytics and no tracking of any kind
- Open source: github.com/mtali/gopods

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

One thing to know: GoPods streams episodes. Saving episodes for offline listening is
not supported yet.
